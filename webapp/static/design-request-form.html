PDF, PNG or JPEG, up to 10 MB. Your file is read by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to fill in the form, and is sent to our engineers with your request. AI can misread values: you'll be asked to check them before submitting.

03

Maximum bearing envelope

The largest footprint and height your structure can accept.

04

Minimum vertical load

The lowest vertical load this bearing could see in service — for example a "Min Vertical" case from your schedule. It decides whether a friction-only Type B bearing is enough, or a positive-fixed Type C is required. Leave it blank and we'll assume 0 kN, and say so clearly in the result.

05
